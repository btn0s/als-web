# Mantle, roll, ragdoll and get up

`Character`'s local input adapter maps jump to ALS jump/mantle probing, Shift sprint/roll policy, and ragdoll/get-up binding. Code-driven players increment `player.command.jumpSequence` once per press and return it from `inputSource`; the fixed physics step consumes each changed sequence once. Return `active:false` on focus loss to clear pending edges. Use `player.ragdoll()` for the ragdoll edge; `player.respawn(position)` recreates the capsule and clears actions. To retry or checkpoint without a remount hitch, pass `Character teleportRef` and call `teleportRef.current.TeleportTo(feet, yawDegrees)`. Like `AActor::TeleportTo`, the next physics step stops ragdoll, mantling and montages, zeroes velocity, places the capsule with its feet at `feet`, sets yaw instantly and marks the animation teleported, so foot locks and camera lag reset. [Teleport bridge](../../../src/als/AlsCharacter.hooks.ts). [Command](../../../src/app/AlsPlayer.ts), [input bridge](../../../src/als/AlsCharacter.hooks.ts), [action tests](../../../src/als/AlsCharacter.hooks.test.ts).

Mantle and roll use authored clips, root motion and notify gates. The local `Character` creates the Rapier mantle adapter and montage bridges; invoke actions through its input route instead of setting `locomotionAction` directly. [Character mount](../../../src/app/Character.tsx), [mantle settings](../../../src/als/Settings/AlsMantlingSettings.ts), [rolling implementation](../../../src/als/AlsCharacter_Actions.ts). `ragdollRef` exposes `StartRagdolling()` and `StopRagdolling()` for a game action policy; get-up is selected by the stop lifecycle. [Ragdoll handle](../../../src/engine/rapierAlsRagdoll.ts), [ragdoll hook](../../../src/app/useAlsRagdoll.ts). Remote action events are cosmetic; see [remote](remote.md).

The public code-input contract currently exposes jump but no explicit roll or mantle request field. The built-in input path applies its Shift roll policy and automatic mantle probe. A controller that needs explicit roll/mantle activation must integrate at the internal action bridge in `src/als/AlsCharacter.hooks.ts` and `src/als/AlsCharacter_Actions.ts`; it cannot produce those actions by assigning `locomotionAction`. For ragdoll/game get-up, call `ragdollRef.current?.StartRagdolling()` and `ragdollRef.current?.StopRagdolling()`.

The mantle adapter distinguishes the contacted collider surface normal from the separating normal of a rounded capsule at a ledge edge. A horizontal sweep that grazes a low ledge may have an upward separating normal even though its contacted face is vertical; the adapter resolves that face on the same collider before applying walkability. The subsequent downward probe still enforces the authored ledge-height range. Real-query regression coverage includes the 0.5 m lower boundary and rejects a 0.49 m ledge from the normal capsule floor clearance.
