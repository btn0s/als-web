# Ground and foot IK

Mount local `Character` inside Rapier `Physics`; its control-rig foot trace queries the scene after movement and before pose publication. Put floors in the same world as the capsule. [Character trace setup](../../../src/app/Character.tsx), [trace adapter](../../../src/engine/rapierAlsControlRigFootTrace.ts), [Control Rig](../../../src/als/AnimGraph/CR_Als.ts).

Remote `<AlsRemoteActor snapshots={buffer} footIk={nearby} />` keeps the authored graph but can skip terrain traces for distant actors. `footIk` defaults true; change it by distance without recreating the actor. [Remote actor](../../../src/app/AlsRemoteActor.tsx), [foot trace tests](../../../src/engine/rapierAlsControlRigFootTrace.test.ts). Floor slope/contact policy belongs to CharacterMovement, not the foot trace.

`<Character terrainFootLock />` opts into terrain stance retention for a local actor. This is an original consumer extension; the default remains the authored ALS foot-lock and Control Rig path. The option uses the current graph pose and `FootPlanted` stance phase (negative left, positive right), or a fully authored `FootLeftLock` / `FootRightLock`, together with a successful terrain trace. A lifted swing foot cannot become a contact solely because its phase curve is nonzero.

The retained ankle target stays in world space through actor translation, yaw and vertical capsule movement over stairs. The pelvis moves into the legs' reachable interval before the two-bone solve, so the reach limiter cannot pull a planted target sideways. The ground trace continues at that contact until toe-off, then its correction blends out over 80 ms. Losing support releases immediately. Airborne movement, locomotion actions and teleports clear the retained contacts. The option currently targets static terrain. Use the default authored movement-base path for moving platforms.

The extension requires full foot IK weight, a phase magnitude of at least 0.5 or a full authored lock, a walkable trace normal with Y at least 0.65, and an authored ankle within 8 cm of reference contact height before touchdown. A terrain height change over 5 cm invalidates an existing anchor. These are original contact validation and release tolerances, not extracted ALS constants. Diagnostic weights `ControlRigFootLeftTerrainLocked` and `ControlRigFootRightTerrainLocked` report retained contacts; published authored curves remain unchanged. Measure the final published foot transforms against the floor and contact intervals when evaluating a consumer's locomotion.
